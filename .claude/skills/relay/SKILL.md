# Relay: post copy, never the post itself

1. **Read** `out/<name>/notes.md` and `out/<name>/captions.srt`, and the brand rules and the client's facts (`mcp__memory__open_nodes` with names ["Brand rules", "Clients"]).
2. **Write** `out/<name>/post.md` with:
   - **Instagram** (in Uzbek): a caption whose first line works on its own, then 2 to 3 short lines, then 5 hashtags at most.
   - **Pinned comment**: one question that invites a real answer.
   - **Telegram** (only if asked): the same idea as a short post with one clear call to action.
3. **Check** every title against the brand rules (length, no clickbait).

End with: "Nothing is posted. Waiting for the owner's OK."
